The student logs into the Registration Portal using their admission credentials.

The student updates their profile information, uploads a passport photograph, and verifies their email address.

Step 3: Dashboard Access

After profile confirmation, the student is redirected to the Dashboard where they can see any ongoing registration for the current semester.

Step 4: Start Registration

The student clicks Continue Registration to begin the semester registration process.

Step 5: Registration Summary

The system displays a registration summary showing the registration details for the selected semester.

Step 6: Payment

The student proceeds to payment. A Remita payment reference is generated and the student completes the payment through the payment gateway.

Step 7: Payment Verification

Once payment is successful, the system verifies the transaction and automatically activates course registration for the student.

Step 8: Add/Drop Courses

The student is redirected to the Add/Drop Courses page where they can select and register their courses for the current semester.

Step 9: Course Registration Submission

The student submits the selected courses and the registration is successfully completed.

Step 10: Registration Record

The registered courses immediately appear on the student’s Registration Page for future reference.

Step 11: Payment History

The successful payment is automatically recorded in the student’s Payment History.

Step 12: Print Documents

The student can print or download their Course Registration Form, Course Selection Slip, and Payment Receipt at any time.

Step 13: Registration Completion

The registration process is completed and all records remain accessible through the student portal.
